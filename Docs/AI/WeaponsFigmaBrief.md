# Weapons — visual brief for Figma

How to use: paste the **Shared style block** plus **one page block** into Figma AI (Make / First Draft) per request.
One page = 10 weapons, so the prompt stays short enough for good results. Ask for 2–3 variants per weapon.
Names are the in-game English names (the game also ships in Russian and 7 other languages — do not put text on the art).

---

## Shared style block (paste every time)

> **Game:** "Rust Carnival" — a post-apocalyptic demolition-derby racer for web and mobile (Yandex Games, WebGL).
> Scavenged cars race through junkyards, wastelands and ruined cities and fight in a dusty arena. Framing: it is a
> SHOW staged by survivors, with sponsor banners and stands built from buses. Camera is a third-person chase camera,
> so weapons are seen small, from above and behind.
>
> **Art style:** chunky stylized low-poly, bright and readable; flat shading, few materials, no photoreal textures.
> Every weapon looks **scavenged and welded together** from salvaged parts — mismatched plates, exposed bolts, hose
> clamps, a hand-stencilled marking — but it is a well-built, working machine, not junk. Tone is gleeful mayhem, never
> grim. Silhouette must read at 48 px.
>
> **Hard content limits:** no blood, no gore, no humans — pilots are repair drones and helmeted mannequins, and cars
> are torn apart instead of people (bolts, sparks and springs fly, never blood). No trademarked iconography
> (no Warhammer 40k eagles or skulls, no Fallout vault imagery, no Marvel Carnage marks). Full art direction:
> `ArtDirection.md`.
>
> **Where the weapon sits:** every weapon is a module bolted onto the car roof or bumper: a small base plate, a
> pivot, and the weapon head. Melee weapons fold on an arm and extend forward/sideways to hit. Traps are dropped
> behind the car and lie on the road. Keep each module roughly the size of the car roof (≈ 1/3 of the car length).
>
> **Colour coding by family** (accent colour on the base plate / trim, body can vary; values are the game's UI tokens):
> - Ballistic guns — gunmetal + hazard orange `#FF8A1F` accent
> - Melee rigs — bare steel + red `#E03B2A` accent
> - Beams & energy — dark body + cyan `#3FE0FF` glow (the game's only clean glow: nitro, plasma, the space gates)
> - Sprays (cone) — tank + nozzle, accent in the effect colour (fire orange, acid lime `#8CC63F`, ice blue, water blue)
> - Pulse (radial burst) — purple/magenta glow
> - Traps — dark plate + 45° yellow/black hazard stripes
> - Funny weapons — saturated candy colours, faces and googly eyes allowed (they are the show's crowd-pleasers)
>
> **What I need for each weapon:**
> 1. A 3/4 top-view concept of the module mounted on a simple generic car (neutral grey car so the weapon stands out).
> 2. A flat icon for the weapon wheel: single silhouette, 2–3 colours, thick outline, transparent background,
>    readable at 48×48 and 96×96, no text.
> 3. A small "in action" sketch (muzzle flash, beam, swing, spray cone, trap triggering).
>
> Give 2–3 different visual variants per weapon. Keep one consistent style across all weapons of this page.
> Lay out as a grid: one row per weapon, columns = variants.
